Time Restorer Privacy Policy
Effective: October 18, 2026

Time Restorer is an AI photo restoration app for iOS and Android. This policy
explains what we process, why we process it, who receives it, and how you can
delete it. The data controller is Neo Guo ("we", "us"), contactable at
guoxinrui1112@gmail.com.


1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

Photos you submit for restoration
This is the core of the service and it works differently from an on-device
editor. When you import a photo from your library or capture one with the
in-app scanner, the image is uploaded from your device to our backend and then
transmitted to Replicate so the restoration can run.
Depending on the mode you choose, this covers colorization of black-and-white
photos, face restoration and enhancement, upscaling and denoising, and repair
of scratches, folds, tears, and stains. Restored results are stored in our
cloud storage so they can be shown in your history and downloaded by you. If
you do not want a photo uploaded, do not submit it for restoration. The in-app
scanner uses on-device edge detection to crop the photo, but the cropped image
is still uploaded when you run a restoration.

Account and authentication data
We use Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous account by default and
to support Sign in with Apple or Google. We process your Firebase user ID, the
authentication provider, and the account identifiers supplied by that provider.
We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

Restoration history
We store the images you submit and the results we generate, together with task
metadata such as mode, timestamps, and status, so that your history is
available across sessions and can be deleted with your account.

Purchases and subscriptions
Apple or Google processes payment details. RevenueCat receives a customer
identifier linked to your Firebase user ID, your entitlement and subscription
status, and store transaction information so we can unlock Pro features. We do
not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Usage, device, and diagnostic data
We process quota usage, app and device type, app version, language, coarse
technical logs, and a randomly generated device identifier. The device
identifier is converted to a one-way hash for quota and abuse-prevention
records. We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage and app
performance, and analytics events may be associated with your Firebase user ID.
We do not send your photos or their contents to analytics.

Advertising data
Free users may see ads supplied by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent and
device settings, AdMob may process advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS
or the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, ad
interactions, and consent status in order to deliver and measure ads. On iOS we
request App Tracking Transparency permission before using the advertising
identifier for personalized advertising. Photos you submit for restoration are
not shared with advertising providers.


2. WHY WE USE INFORMATION

- Provide accounts, upload and process photos, return restored results, and
  display your history.
- Verify subscriptions, maintain accurate restoration allowances, prevent
  abuse, and provide customer support.
- Measure app performance and feature use, fix errors, and improve
  Time Restorer.
- Show contextual or personalized ads, measure their performance, and honor
  your privacy choices.
- Comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect users and the service.


3. SHARING AND SERVICE PROVIDERS

We share data only as needed to run the service. Our current providers include
Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, backend, storage, and
analytics), Replicate (AI photo restoration), RevenueCat (subscription
management), and Google AdMob (advertising). Providers that process photos do
so only to return a result for your request, and they handle data under their
own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose data if required by law,
to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or sale. We do not sell
personal information.


4. RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

We keep account records, uploaded photos, and restored results while your
account is active and as reasonably needed to provide the service. You can
delete your Time Restorer account in the app: open Account, tap Delete Account,
and confirm. This deletes the Firebase account and the server records we
associate with it, including uploaded photos, restored results, quota and
entitlement records, AI job records, and reward records, and it requests
deletion of the related RevenueCat customer record. Some records may remain
temporarily in encrypted backups, or be retained where law, fraud prevention,
or store transaction rules require it. Deleting the app does not delete your
account. If you cannot use the in-app option, email us from the address
associated with your account and we will verify and process the request.


5. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

- Change photo, camera, and tracking permissions in your device settings.
- Manage personalized advertising through iOS Tracking settings, Android Ads
  settings, or the in-app privacy-options entry where available.
- Request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion by emailing
  guoxinrui1112@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity.
- Cancel a subscription in Apple or Google, separately from deleting your
  account.


6. SECURITY AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access
controls, App Check, encrypted transport, and provider security controls. No
system is perfectly secure. Our providers may process data in countries other
than yours, with safeguards required by applicable law.


7. CHILDREN

Time Restorer is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age
where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.


8. CHANGES AND CONTACT

We may update this policy and will post the new effective date here. Material
changes will be communicated in the app or by another appropriate method.
Questions or privacy requests: guoxinrui1112@gmail.com
