Good questions are part of the process

Before you begin

Is InkFlow a tattoo generator or a design tool? ＋

InkFlow is designed as a creative direction tool. It helps you explore concepts, styles, and body placement so you have something more useful than a random image — a direction you can discuss with a tattoo artist.

What can Time Restorer do for an old photo? ＋

Time Restorer lets you scan or import a photo, colorize black-and-white memories, restore faces and faded details, compare the original with the result, and save the restored image to your history.

Do I need to know exactly what I want? ＋

No. Both products are built for the in-between stage — when you know the feeling before you know the final form.
