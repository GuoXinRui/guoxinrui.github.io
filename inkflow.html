Questions

Is InkFlow a tattoo generator or a design tool? ＋

InkFlow is a creative direction tool. It helps you explore concepts, styles, and body placement so you have something more useful than a random image — a direction you can discuss with a tattoo artist.

Can I see a tattoo on my own body before I commit? ＋

Yes. You can place a direction on a photo of your own arm, back, or leg to judge scale and placement. The body preview is generated on your device.

Do my body-preview photos leave my device? ＋

No. Photos you choose for the body preview are processed locally and are not uploaded to our servers or to any AI provider. They are saved to your device photo library only when you explicitly tap Save.

How many generations do I get for free? ＋

New users get 3 free generations that do not expire. Pro plans add a recurring allowance: 50 per week on the weekly plan, 200 per month on the monthly plan, and 300 per month on the yearly plan. Unused recurring credits reset at the end of each allowance period.

Will InkFlow produce exactly what my artist tattoos? ＋

Not necessarily, and it is not meant to. AI output is a starting point for a conversation. A qualified tattoo artist adapts a direction to your skin, the placement, and their own technique. Always consult a professional before getting tattooed.
